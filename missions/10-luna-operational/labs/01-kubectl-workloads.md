# LAB 01 — KUBECTL & WORKLOADS

Use a disposable namespace.

Requirements:

1. Create a namespace.
2. Apply a Deployment from YAML.
3. Run two replicas.
4. Inspect Pods.
5. Describe one Pod.
6. View logs.
7. Delete one Pod.
8. Observe automatic replacement.
9. Scale to three replicas.
10. Scale back to one.
11. Use `kubectl top`.
12. Delete the namespace.

Explain:

```text
Pod
Deployment
ReplicaSet
desired state
```
